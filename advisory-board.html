Advisory Board

The Experience Behind How We Build and Grow

Our Advisory Board brings decades of experience building, scaling and leading Canadian health technology companies to every stage of RevelleCare's growth.

About Dorothy Spence

Member · Advisory Board

Dorothy Spence

  • Founder & CEO, Imaginal Ventures Inc.
  • Co-founder, President & CEO, TecKnowledge Healthcare Systems (1993–2001)
  • Halifax, Nova Scotia

Welcoming Dorothy Spence to the RevelleCare Advisory Board

I am delighted to welcome Dorothy Spence, Founder and CEO of Imaginal Ventures, to RevelleCare as a member of our Advisory Board.

When I set out to build RevelleCare, I knew our advisors would need more than great ideas. I needed leaders who have built in this space before: people who understand what it takes to bring healthcare to Canadians through technology, and to grow a company from an early vision into a national business. Dorothy is exactly that leader.

A pioneer in Canadian digital health

Long before “telehealth” was part of everyday language, Dorothy was building it. From 1993 to 2001, she was co-founder, President and CEO of TecKnowledge Healthcare Systems, which became Canada's leading telehealth company, with offices and networks across the country. TecKnowledge built videoconferencing and medical-imaging networks that let hospitals and rural communities connect patients with care, share diagnostic images and deliver continuing education. Its work included a province-wide network linking hospitals across Nova Scotia, as well as projects in Ontario, New Brunswick and at the IWK Health Centre in Halifax.

Dorothy built, scaled and led a national health technology company, securing capital, recruiting teams with both healthcare and technical skills, and earning the trust of health systems across Canada. It is the same journey RevelleCare is on today.

A builder of scaling companies

As Founder and CEO of Imaginal Ventures in Halifax, she has supported more than 300 organizations across Canada, the United States and Europe through expansion, restructuring and investment readiness. An engineer by training, she created the Conscious Business Operating System™, was one of the architects of Canada Health Infoway and is a founding member of the Canadian Telehealth Society. She also created the Trust Diagnostic, which helps leadership teams build the systems, culture and trust that sustainable growth depends on.

Her leadership has been recognized with a Top CEO Award, an Innovation Award and the Professional Engineers Service Award. She has championed women-led and Atlantic Canadian ventures through programs with partners including TD and Spring, and she shares her thinking on leadership as host of Trust Is Not a KPI.

What Dorothy brings to RevelleCare

Dorothy brings what every growing digital health company needs most: someone who has done it before. She has built a Canadian health technology company from the ground up, and she has guided hundreds of founders through the stages RevelleCare is entering now.

Dorothy, thank you for believing in RevelleCare and in the women we are building it for. We are so glad to have you with us.

Michèle Lunney
Founder & CEO, RevelleCare Inc.
