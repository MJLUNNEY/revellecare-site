Advisory Board

The Experience Behind How We Build and Grow

Our Advisory Board brings decades of experience building, scaling and leading Canadian health technology companies to every stage of RevelleCare's growth.

Announcement

We are delighted to announce that Dorothy Spence — Founder and CEO of Imaginal Ventures and co-founder of TecKnowledge Healthcare Systems, Canada's leading telehealth company — has joined RevelleCare as a founding member of our Advisory Board.

Dorothy is one of Canada's true pioneers in digital health. Long before virtual care was part of everyday life, she co-founded TecKnowledge and led it as President and CEO, building telehealth networks that connected hospitals and rural communities with care across the country. In the decades since, she has helped hundreds of founders build, scale and grow their companies — guided by the same conviction RevelleCare was founded on: that technology, built deliberately and responsibly, can bring better care within reach.

Her appointment comes at a pivotal moment for the company. As RevelleCare moves through pre-launch clinical and pharmacy workflow testing toward our Canadian launch in early 2027, Dorothy will advise on our business plan and operating model, our investor readiness and seed raise, and the leadership, systems and culture we need to scale with discipline.

Dorothy has already done what we are setting out to do. She built a Canadian health technology company from the ground up and brought care to communities through technology long before anyone called it virtual care. Since then, she has guided hundreds of founders through exactly the stage RevelleCare is entering now. Having her in our corner gives me — and every woman we are building RevelleCare for — confidence that we are building this company the right way.”

— Michèle Lunney, Founder & CEO, RevelleCare Inc.

About Dorothy Spence

Member · Advisory Board

Dorothy Spence

  • Founder & CEO, Imaginal Ventures Inc.
  • Co-founder, President & CEO, TecKnowledge Healthcare Systems (1993–2001)
  • Halifax, Nova Scotia

Dorothy is an engineer, serial entrepreneur, author and speaker whose career has been built at the meeting point of technology, healthcare and growth. She earned her engineering degree from Dalhousie University, where she was one of four women in a graduating class of 80. From 1993 to 2001, she was co-founder, President and CEO of TecKnowledge Healthcare Systems, which became Canada's leading telehealth company, with offices and networks across the country. TecKnowledge's videoconferencing and medical-imaging networks allowed care teams to consult with patients, share diagnostic images and deliver continuing education at a distance — including a province-wide network linking hospitals across Nova Scotia and telehealth projects in Ontario, New Brunswick and at the IWK Health Centre in Halifax. As Founder and CEO of Imaginal Ventures, she has supported more than 300 organizations across Canada, the United States and Europe through expansion, restructuring and investment readiness, and created the Conscious Business Operating System™ and the Trust Pulse diagnostic to help leadership teams scale with performance and trust. She was named the Canadian coach for SheEO, has received the Top CEO Award, an Innovation Award and the Professional Engineers Service Award, and hosts Trust Is Not a KPI.

Dorothy's Role at RevelleCare

Beyond credentials, what matters is what an advisor actually does. Dorothy's contributions to RevelleCare are specific, ongoing and focused on how we build, fund and grow the company.

Business plan & operating model
Helps refine RevelleCare's strategy, care and staffing model, go-to-market plan and five-year financial forecast.
Investor readiness
Sharpens our investor narrative and materials, prepares us for investor meetings and due diligence, and supports our search for a lead investor.
Scaling & leadership
Guides the leadership team, systems and culture RevelleCare needs to grow from launch to scale with discipline.
Digital health experience
Brings first-hand experience building and delivering healthcare through technology across Canada, and working alongside the health systems and partners that make it possible.
Working cadence
Meets weekly with RevelleCare's Founder & CEO, with regular reviews of progress against agreed goals and milestones.
Term
Initial appointment through June 2027, renewable by mutual agreement of the parties.
