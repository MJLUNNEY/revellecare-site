    RevelleCare is a telemedicine platform that facilitates access to independent licensed healthcare practitioners and a licensed compounding pharmacy. RevelleCare does not practice medicine, does not provide medical advice, and is not a pharmacy or a drug manufacturer. All medical decisions — including whether to prescribe — are made solely by your licensed clinician based on medical necessity. Prescriptions are prepared and dispensed only by a licensed pharmacist. This website does not advertise or promote any prescription drug and is not a substitute for professional medical advice.

    A prescription is issued only after you complete a medical assessment and consultation with a licensed clinician, and only if the clinician determines treatment is medically appropriate for you.
